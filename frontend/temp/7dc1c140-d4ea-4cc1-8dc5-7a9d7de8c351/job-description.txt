About Perfect Harvest

Perfect Harvest LLC is a leading management provider in the cannabis industry that focuses primarily on cannabis cultivation. We design, operate, and consult for facilities across the country and pride ourselves on working alongside industry leaders. We are seeking driven, detail oriented, and positive individuals to join our team. Join our team today to experience personal growth and career opportunities in an expanding industry!


The Role

We are seeking a highly skilled Full Stack Developer to join our team in a pivotal role focused on enhancing operational efficiency and integrating AI-driven technologies directly into our business processes. Working closely with our CTO and technical team, you will develop and optimize solutions that drive profitability, automation, and scalability across our operations.


Responsibilities

    Develop and maintain serverless applications using Firebase Functions and Google Cloud Platform.
    Implement and manage NoSQL and SQL databases, ensuring data integrity and performance.
    Collaborate with product and design teams to create intuitive and responsive user interfaces using modern JavaScript frameworks.
    Utilize TypeScript for building robust and type-safe applications.
    Write and maintain efficient, reusable, and reliable code.
    Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to peers.
    Troubleshoot and resolve application issues, ensuring high performance and responsiveness.
    Participate in Agile development processes, contributing to sprint planning and retrospectives.


Key Technologies:

    Proficiency in JavaScript frameworks such as React, Angular, or Vue.js.
    Experience with Node.js, Express, and Firebase Functions.
    Strong knowledge of NoSQL Databases (Mongo DB, Firestore, etc) and SQL databases (mySQL, PostgreSQL, etc)
    Proficiency in TypeScript and JavaScript.
    Familiarity with date-fns for date manipulation and Prisma for database management.
    Experience with Google Cloud Platform and serverless architecture.
    Proficient in using Git for version control.


Qualifications:

    Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
    Proven experience as a Full Stack Developer or similar role.
    Strong understanding of serverless architecture and cloud services.
    Experience with RESTful APIs and third-party integrations.
    Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.
    Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
