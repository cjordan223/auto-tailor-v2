Company: NovaScale Infrastructure
Position: Systems Engineer

Job Description:
NovaScale Infrastructure provides scalable and resilient cloud solutions for enterprise clients. We are looking for a Systems Engineer to design, build, and maintain the core infrastructure that powers our services. The ideal candidate has a strong background in Linux systems administration, automation, and cloud technologies. You will be instrumental in ensuring the reliability, scalability, and performance of our production environments.

Responsibilities:
* Manage and maintain our hybrid-cloud infrastructure, primarily based on Linux systems.
* Develop and manage infrastructure as code (IaC) using Terraform and Ansible to automate provisioning and configuration.
* Administer our container orchestration platform (Kubernetes) and associated tooling (e.g., Helm, Prometheus).
* Implement and manage monitoring, logging, and alerting systems to ensure high availability and performance.
* Troubleshoot and resolve complex infrastructure issues, performing root cause analysis to prevent recurrence.
* Collaborate with software engineers to optimize application performance and reliability.
* Manage system security, including patch management, hardening, and access control.
* Participate in an on-call rotation to support our 24/7 production environment.

Required Qualifications:
* Bachelor's degree in a relevant technical field or equivalent practical experience.
* 3-5 years of experience in a Systems Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer, or similar role.
* Deep expertise in Linux/UNIX systems administration and troubleshooting.
* Hands-on experience with configuration management tools like Ansible, Puppet, or Chef.
* Strong experience with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes).
* Proficiency in a scripting language such as Python, Go, or Bash.
* Experience with cloud providers like AWS, Azure, or GCP.

Desired Qualifications:
* Experience with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins or GitLab CI.
* Familiarity with networking concepts (TCP/IP, DNS, VPNs, BGP).
* Knowledge of database administration (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL).
* Experience building and maintaining highly available, fault-tolerant systems.
