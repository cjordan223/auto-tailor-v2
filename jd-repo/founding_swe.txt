Responsibilities

You will develop and improve our AI systems, including:

    Fine-tuning large language models on real estate-specific workflows and communications for workflows like listing description generation, client response drafting, task management, and lead follow-up.
    Building evaluation frameworks to ensure high-quality and safe model outputs.
    Designing and implementing novel search and question-answering algorithms over real estate data (e.g., listings, market trends, CRM activity).
    Collecting, structuring, and indexing fragmented industry datasets from MLSs, brokerage platforms, and public sources to support agents with MLS search, pricing analysis, comp generation, and deal insights.
    Building and orchestrating large systems of interconnected LLMs to power end-to-end agent workflows,
    Solving challenging engineering problems around low-latency interactions, expensive model inference, and tool/model integration.


Impact
