Company: UrbanGo Transit
Position: Full Stack Engineer

Job Description:
UrbanGo is a mobile-first company revolutionizing urban transportation. Our app helps millions of commuters navigate their cities with real-time transit data. We are looking for a versatile Full Stack Engineer with a passion for mobile development to join our core product team. You will build and maintain features across our entire stack, from our React Native mobile app to our Go-powered backend APIs.

Responsibilities:
* Develop, test, and ship new user-facing features in our React Native mobile application for both iOS and Android.
* Design, build, and maintain scalable and performant backend services and APIs using Go.
* Collaborate with product managers and designers to translate product requirements into technical solutions.
* Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code across the full stack.
* Work with our PostgreSQL database, designing schemas and writing efficient queries.
* Optimize application performance, focusing on mobile responsiveness and API latency.
* Participate in code reviews and contribute to a high-quality, collaborative engineering culture.
* Debug production issues and support the services you build.

Required Qualifications:
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience.
* 3+ years of professional software development experience.
* Proficiency in at least one modern backend language (e.g., Go, Node.js, Python) and one frontend framework.
* Experience developing mobile applications with React Native, or native experience with Swift/Kotlin and a strong desire to learn.
* Experience building and consuming RESTful APIs.
* Familiarity with relational databases like PostgreSQL or MySQL.
* Proficient with Git and modern software development workflows.

Desired Qualifications:
* Strong experience with Go (Golang) for backend development.
* Experience with cloud platforms like GCP or AWS, and containerization with Docker.
* Knowledge of mobile-specific challenges like offline storage, push notifications, and performance optimization.
* Experience with mapping technologies or geospatial data.
