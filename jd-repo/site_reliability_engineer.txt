Company: StreamFlow Media
Position: Site Reliability Engineer

Job Description:
StreamFlow Media operates a global video streaming platform serving millions of concurrent users. We're looking for a Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) who is passionate about building reliable, scalable, and performant distributed systems. You will apply software engineering principles to solve infrastructure and operations problems, with a focus on automation, observability, and incident response to maintain our strict service level objectives (SLOs).

Responsibilities:
* Develop and implement software to improve the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of our services.
* Define and monitor Service Level Indicators (SLIs) and Service Level Objectives (SLOs) for critical systems.
* Lead incident response, conduct blameless post-mortems, and drive follow-up actions to prevent future outages.
* Build and maintain robust observability tooling for monitoring, logging, and tracing (e.g., Prometheus, Jaeger, Grafana).
* Automate manual operational tasks, from deployments and configuration changes to failure mitigation.
* Engage in capacity planning and performance analysis to ensure our platform can handle future growth.
* Participate in a 24/7 on-call rotation to support the production environment.
* Work with development teams to ensure new features are designed with reliability and scalability in mind.

Required Qualifications:
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related technical field.
* 3-5 years of experience in SRE, DevOps, or software engineering with a focus on infrastructure.
* Strong programming skills in a language like Go, Python, or Java.
* Experience managing large-scale, distributed systems on a public cloud platform (GCP, AWS).
* Deep understanding of Linux operating systems and networking.
* Experience with container orchestration systems, especially Kubernetes.
* A systematic, data-driven approach to problem-solving.

Desired Qualifications:
* Experience with chaos engineering principles and tools.
* Familiarity with Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) and video streaming protocols.
* Experience with distributed databases and caching systems.
* A strong desire to eliminate toil through automation.
